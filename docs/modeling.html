Modeling

Direct 3D CAD modeling: sketch closed profiles that instantly become planar faces, extrude solids, sculpt features, and inspect parametric properties.

Parametric Primitives

Select a primitive from the left dock (or press its hotkey) and click in the viewport to define its position and dimensions. Primitives remain fully parametric in the Properties inspector.

2D Sketching & Face Creation

Sketch tools draw 2D profiles with live grid snapping. Connecting curve endpoints into a closed loop automatically produces a shaded planar face ready for extrusion.

2D Sketch Tools
ToolKeyBehavior
LineLTwo-point line. Hold Shift for orthogonal lock. Chains from endpoints.
PolylineJClick to place vertices. Click the start vertex or press Enter to close.
RectangleRTwo-corner rectangle. Hold Shift for square. Auto-creates a planar face.
CircleCCenter and radius. Auto-creates an extrudable planar face.
ArcAThree-point arc: start point, end point, and bulge point.
PolygonNRegular polygon centered at point. Hold Shift to unlock rotation.

Solid & Face Tools

Transform 2D sketch faces into 3D bodies and modify solid geometry directly:

Solid & Modification Tools
ToolKeyAction
Extrude / Extrude FaceEClick any closed planar face or profile and drag upward or downward to produce a solid body.
Fillet / Fillet EdgeFRound corners on 2D sketch polylines or smooth sharp edges on 3D solid bodies.
OffsetOOffset curves by a specific distance inward or outward.
RevolveWSpin an open or closed profile around an axis by a specified angle.
SweepSSweep a profile along a 3D path curve.
LoftKCreate a continuous solid transition through two or more closed profiles.
TrimTDelete the crossed segment between two intersecting curves.
ExtendXExtend a curve endpoint until it meets the next boundary curve.
MirrorIMirror selected geometry across a two-point reflection axis.

Select & Transform

  • Select (V): Click entities or drag a selection marquee to inspect geometry.
  • Move (M): Transform selected objects with the 3D translation gizmo.
  • Rotate & Scale: Interactive rotation rings and scaling handles.
  • Commit or Deselect: Press Enter to commit current operations or Esc to clear selections.
  • Selection HUD: Floating pill dock at the bottom of the viewport with fast access to Extrude, Fillet, Move, and Delete.
  • Duplicate: Ctrl D; Select All: Ctrl A.

Layers & Project Outline

The right dock houses the Project tree and Layers manager. Create layers with custom colors, toggle global visibility, or lock layers to prevent accidental modifications. Individual entities can inherit or override layer styles.

Undo & Redo

All operations—including parametric property changes and Lua script actions—are recorded in a non-destructive undo stack. Press Ctrl Z to undo and Ctrl Shift Z (or Ctrl Y) to redo.
